# Roadmap

1. Architecture and transparent Section 479 screening foundation (current prototype).
2. Persistent schema, migrations, authentication, server-side RBAC and audit history.
3. Custody documents, validated OCR pipeline, conflict handling and superintendent application review.
4. Bail-to-release tracking and ArrestGuard records.
5. Family Connect, kiosk, juvenile safeguards and settlement review.
6. Evidence file validation, SHA-256, derivatives, provenance and limited explainable screening.
7. Hearing readiness, notifications and governance analytics.
8. Integration, security, accessibility, performance and deployment verification.

No external integration or AI model is currently configured. OCR, forensic classifiers, speech services, notifications and court integrations remain unimplemented.
